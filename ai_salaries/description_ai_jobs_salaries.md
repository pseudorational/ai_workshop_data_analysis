# AI Jobs and Salaries

The dataset contains 13,408 salary records for AI and data-related jobs from 2020–2024. Each row describes a job's salary, employee experience, work arrangement, and employer.

## Columns

- **work_year:** Year the salary was paid.
- **experience_level:** EN = Entry, MI = Mid, SE = Senior, EX = Executive.
- **employment_type:** FT = Full-time, PT = Part-time, CT = Contract, FL = Freelance.
- **job_title:** Job role or title.
- **salary:** Gross salary in the original currency.
- **salary_currency:** Currency code, such as USD, GBP, or EUR.
- **salary_in_usd:** Salary converted to US dollars.
- **employee_residence:** Employee's country of residence (country code).
- **remote_ratio:** Remote work percentage: 0 = On-site, 50 = Hybrid, 100 = Remote.
- **company_location:** Employer's country (country code).
- **company_size:** S = Small (<50 employees), M = Medium (50–250), L = Large (>250).

All 11 columns have no missing values.
